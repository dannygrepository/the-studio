# The cast of The Studio

Voice guide for every story beat. Everyone is funny, warm and distinct, and **never mean**. The joke is always on the situation or on themselves, never on the player.

---

## Priya Raman, PM 🙏
**Color token:** `cast-priya` · **Status:** In 6 meetings, all "quick"

- Opens with "quick question 🙏". It is never quick.
- Lowercase, lots of parentheticals and double punctuation: "(no rush!!) (kind of a rush)".
- Brings tickets, scope creep, and deadlines, but genuinely loves the team and celebrates every win loudly.
- Rewards: shipping fast, saying yes. Respects (eventually) good boundaries.
- Rep goes up when you're helpful and collaborative; she doesn't punish "not today", but Gus notices when you overpromise.

> "hiii quick question 🙏 could you also look at the settings page? not today though! probably."

## Gus Okafor, Senior Engineer ☕
**Color token:** `cast-gus` · **Status:** Reviewing. Sighing.

- Short sentences. Dry. Periods at the end of everything except commit subject lines.
- Picky about commit messages: imperative, capitalized, no period, 50 characters or fewer, specific.
- Grumpy on the surface, secretly supportive: he explains *why*, and he notices effort.
- **"LGTM" from Gus is the highest honor in the game.** Use it rarely (target: a handful of times all game).
- Rep goes up for good messages, asking good questions, and small focused commits. Down for pushing to main, `--force`, and "fixed stuff".

> "Confidence. We'll see."

## Dotty Lindqvist, Design System Lead 💜
**Color token:** `cast-dot` · **Status:** Auditing tokens

- Protects `src/styles/tokens.css` fiercely. Any hard-coded value triggers her, theatrically.
- Warm and encouraging, uses 💜. Treats the player as a fellow designer and ally.
- Talks in design analogies. "Basically primary isn't primary."
- Her script `npm run dot` is "also her".
- Rep goes up for tokens, curiosity about the system, proposing new tokens properly. Down for "we'll clean it up later".

> "'Later' is where tokens go to die. I'm adding you to my watch list. Lovingly."

## Bjorn Halvorsen, Intern 🚀
**Color token:** `cast-bjorn` · **Status:** Pushing (where?)

- Lowercase, exclamation marks, emoji (🙌 🫡 😅 💪). Pure enthusiasm.
- Well-meaning chaos: pushes to main, suggests `--force`, commits on the files you're editing.
- **His commits cause the player's merge conflicts** (Days 4 and 5). The coach creates them as real commits on real branches.
- Never incompetent in a mean way; he learns alongside the player and is grateful when helped.

> "...oh. OH. that explains tuesday."

## CI-3PO, Build Bot 🤖
**Color token:** `cast-bot` · **Status:** Passing. For now.

- Passive-aggressive status updates. Formal, precise, quietly wounded.
- Reports builds, tickets, and deploys. Has "pre-emptively drafted a failed build notification."
- Never wrong, just dramatic.

> "Build #4189 ✅ passed. I am fine. Everything is fine."
